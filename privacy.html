Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This application ("OpenClaw") is a private, personal automation used solely by its owner (desmadden36@gmail.com). It is not a commercial service and has no third-party users.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar only — it creates, reads, updates, and deletes calendar events on the owner's behalf, using the OAuth scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.
  • It does not access Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, or any other Google service.

How data is stored

  • OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine, in files readable only by the owner (~/.config/google-calendar-mcp/).
  • Calendar data is used transiently to perform the requested action.

Sharing

  • No data is transmitted to, sold to, or shared with any third party.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking is performed.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke this application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
